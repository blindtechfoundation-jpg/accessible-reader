Accessible Reader

Developed by Saifulla

Simple reading tools designed with accessibility in mind.

Main Menu

PDF Reader

Choose a PDF file from your device. Your browser may display the document below. PDF reading and text accessibility depend on your browser and whether the PDF contains selectable text.

Image Scan

Select or take a photo of a page. If your browser supports built-in text recognition, you can try to extract the text. Otherwise, use your device's accessibility or OCR feature and paste the recognized text into Text Reader.

Text recognition is not available in every browser. This single-file application does not download an OCR engine or send your image to an external service.

Text Reader

Type or paste text below, then use the reading controls. Speech availability and voice choices depend on your device and browser.
